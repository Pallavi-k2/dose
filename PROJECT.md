# Half-Life

*Interaction Design*

A caffeine tracker concept for Apple Watch that shows how much you've had today against a personal limit set from Apple Health, designed and prototyped end-to-end, from interaction model to motion and sound.

**Short version:** An Apple Watch concept for tracking caffeine against a personal, Health-based daily limit, prototyped with liquid motion, a tick-ring dial and synthesized sound.
